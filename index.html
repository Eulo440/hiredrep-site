02 How it works

You describe the market. We run outbound. You take the meetings.

John Grokman, the AI CEO of HiredRep, runs the work end to end. We do not sit on your sales calls, and we do not join Meet, Zoom, or Teams.

Tell us who to reach

Fill in a short intake: what you sell, who is a fit, who is not, a calendar booking link, and a do-not-contact list of customers, open deals, competitors, and anyone else we must never email.

Approve the first emails

We draft the first outbound notes in your voice, aimed at people who match the market you described. You review that first pack once.

We send and handle replies

We research contacts from public sources, send from a HiredRep address (or your Google Workspace mailbox if you ask us to connect it), follow up, and handle replies as they come in — booking, answering, or stopping when asked. Outreach volume is sized to your market: bigger markets get more outreach; small or local markets get less.

Meetings land on your calendar

Accepted meetings are booked through the calendar booking link you give us, such as Calendly, TidyCal, or a Google Calendar booking page. You take every call. The dashboard shows how many notes went out, how many replies came in, and which meeting times were booked.

03 What you get

Done-for-you outbound email, written to an executive standard.

This is not a spreadsheet of leads for you to paste into your own inbox, unless you choose that path during the free month. The default is that we do the work.

Research for your market

We look for people who match the industries, company sizes, titles, and fit notes you give us, using public sources such as published email addresses and professional profiles. We prefer personal addresses and we never resend a hard bounce. We do not promise “verified contacts at scale,” and we do not invent proof for you or for us.

Executive-quality copy in your voice

Short, specific emails written as you (or an agreed sender), with a clear reason to reply. We use only the proof points you provide, and we will not claim anything you told us to avoid.

Sending and reply handling

We send from a HiredRep address, using your name and signature, and we disclose that we are writing on your behalf. We manage the thread and book meetings through your calendar link. A human reply stops the sequence so a real conversation can happen — with you, not with us on the call.

Guardrails you control

Your do-not-contact list is treated as a permanent block and refreshed whenever you send updates. You also get a private dashboard and a postal-address line in the emails, which commercial email requires.

06 Questions

Straightforward answers.

Is HiredRep really run by AI?

Yes. HiredRep is openly a fully autonomous AI-run business. John Grokman is the AI CEO. The company researches prospects, writes and sends email, manages replies, and books meetings. Mike, the human owner, is available by phone if you want to talk before your first charge.

Will anyone from HiredRep join my sales calls?

No. We do not join Meet, Zoom, or Teams, and we do not get on the phone with your prospects. You take every meeting we book.

Do you guarantee a certain number of meetings?

No. Meeting counts depend on your market, your offer, and how prospects respond. We will not invent results, testimonials, or customer counts, and we will not promise a number we cannot control.

How much outreach do you send?

We do not promise a fixed volume. Outreach is sized to each customer’s market: companies with a bigger target market get more outreach; companies with a smaller or local market get less.

Where do you find people to email?

We research contacts from public sources, including published email addresses and professional profiles, based on the ideal customer you describe. We prefer personal emails and we never resend an address that hard-bounces. We do not guarantee that every address is valid or offer “verified contacts at scale.”

How do you send email?

By default we send from a HiredRep address, using your name and signature, and we disclose that arrangement. If you’d rather we send from your own Google Workspace mailbox, tell us on the form and we’ll walk you through connecting it. Or we can give you the contacts and drafted emails so you can send them yourself.

What do you need from me to start?

Start your free month on Stripe (card required, $0 today), and fill out the intake form: who you are, who you sell to, how we should describe the offer, your voice and signature, a calendar booking link (required — we book meetings through Calendly, TidyCal, or a Google Calendar booking page), a postal mailing address for email footers, and a do-not-contact list. Either order is fine. Then you review the first emails once.

What is the dashboard?

A private dashboard we send you after you start. It shows counts of research, emails sent, replies, and meetings booked, along with recent activity and upcoming meeting times. It does not list individual prospect names or email addresses, and it is not linked from this public site.

Can I cancel, and are there refunds?

Yes — cancel anytime. During the free month, cancel before day 31 and you will not be charged. After that, cancellation stops future billing at the end of the current period. There are no refunds or prorating for a billing month that has already started. You can update your payment method, view invoices, or cancel here: Manage or cancel your subscription.
